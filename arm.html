Engineering Assignment

6-DOF Robotic Arm

3D Design using Fusion 360

A detailed 3D model of a six-degree-of-freedom robotic arm designed using Fusion 360, focusing on mechanical structure, joint planning and realistic motion.

01

Introduction

This project involved the 3D design of a 6-Degree of Freedom (6-DOF) robotic arm using Fusion 360.

The model replicates the articulated movement of industrial robotic arms commonly used in manufacturing, packaging, and high-precision tasks.

02

Project Highlights

Joint Movement Base, Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist & Gripper
Design Approach Parametric CAD Design
Joint Alignment Precise Axis Constraints
Implementation Designed for Servo Compatibility
03

Design Workflow

01

Base

Designed a stable rotating platform with mounting holes.

02

Shoulder & Elbow

Built hollow structural links to allow space for motors.

03

Wrist & Gripper

Modeled a parallel-jaw end effector for object handling.

04

Assembly

Applied Joint, Revolute and As-Built Joint tools to replicate realistic motion.

Conclusion

This project enhanced my skills in mechanical design, CAD-based joint planning, and modeling for motion.

In future phases, I aim to animate this robotic arm within Fusion 360 or control it using physical hardware such as microcontrollers and servo motors.
